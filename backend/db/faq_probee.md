## GeM Registration

### Q1. What is GeM?
**Question:** What is GeM?
**Also asked as:** GeM kya hai? | What is GeM portal? | GeM ka matlab kya hai? | Tell me about GeM
**Answer:** GeM stands for Government e-Marketplace. It is an online platform used by government departments and organizations for procurement of products and services. ProBid Consultants LLP provides assistance with GeM seller registration, profile setup, product/service listing and other GeM-related requirements.

### Q2. How can I register as a seller on GeM?
**Question:** How can I register as a seller on GeM?

**Answer:** To become a seller on GeM, you generally need to create a seller account and provide the required business and identity information. Depending on your business and the products or services you want to offer, additional documents or verification may be required. ProBid can assist you with the registration process and guide you through the required documentation.

## Government Tender

### Q10. What is a government tender?
**Question:** What is a government tender?
**Also asked as:** Sarkari tender kya hai? | What is tender? | Government tender ka matlab?
**Answer:** A government tender is a formal procurement process through which a government department or organization invites eligible businesses to submit offers for supplying products, providing services or executing works. Businesses must meet the tender's eligibility and technical requirements before submitting a bid.

### Q11. How can I find government tenders?
**Question:** How can I find government tenders?
**Also asked as:** How to find government tenders? | Sarkari tender kaise dhunde? | Where to search tenders? | Tender kahan milega?
**Answer:** Government tenders can be published on platforms such as GeM and government e-procurement portals. You can search based on your product, service, department, location or other tender requirements. ProBid can assist businesses in identifying and evaluating relevant tender opportunities.

### Q12. How do I know whether I am eligible for a tender?
**Question:** How do I know whether I am eligible for a tender?
**Also asked as:** How to check tender eligibility? | Tender eligibility kaise check kare? | Am I eligible for this tender?
**Answer:** Tender eligibility is determined by the conditions mentioned in the tender document. These conditions may include requirements related to: Business registration, Product or service specifications, Experience, Turnover, Technical capability, Certifications, OEM authorization, Financial requirements, Other documents specified by the buyer. ProBid can help review the tender requirements and identify the documents and eligibility conditions applicable to your business.

### Q13. What documents are required for government tender bidding?
**Question:** What documents are required for government tender bidding?
**Also asked as:** Tender bidding ke liye kaun se documents chahiye? | Tender documents list? | Bid submit karne ke liye kya kagaz chahiye?
**Answer:** The documents depend on the specific tender. Common requirements may include: PAN, GST documents, Business registration documents, Bank details, Technical documents, Financial documents, Experience certificates, OEM authorization, Product specifications, Declarations and undertakings, Digital Signature Certificate where required. The tender document should always be treated as the final source for the specific bid.

### Q14. Can a new company participate in government tenders?
**Question:** Can a new company participate in government tenders?
**Also asked as:** Kya nayi company tender bhar sakti hai? | New company tender eligibility?
**Answer:** A new company can participate in tenders where it meets the stated eligibility conditions. However, some tenders may require previous experience, minimum turnover or other qualifications. Therefore, eligibility needs to be checked separately for each tender.

### Q15. Can I participate in a tender without previous experience?
**Question:** Can I participate in a tender without previous experience?
**Also asked as:** Bina experience ke tender bhar sakte hai? | Tender without experience possible?
**Answer:** It depends on the tender. Some tenders may have experience requirements, while others may not. Certain applicable exemptions or special conditions may also exist. ProBid can review the tender document and explain the experience requirements applicable to that tender.

### Q16. How do I submit a government tender?
**Question:** How do I submit a government tender?
**Also asked as:** How to submit a tender? | Tender kaise submit kare? | Bid submission process?
**Answer:** The process generally involves: Finding the relevant tender, Reading the complete tender document, Checking eligibility, Preparing technical documents, Preparing the financial bid/BOQ, Arranging required declarations and certificates, Completing the online submission, Submitting the bid before the deadline. The exact process depends on the procurement portal and tender.
